ABOUT AURA SOURCE LIMITED

Creating fragrance with purpose.

WHO WE ARE

More than a fragrance company.

Aura Source Limited is a Nigerian consumer products company focused on fragrance, freshness and everyday personal care.

Our vision is to create products that make people feel confident, comfortable and connected to the spaces around them.

From perfumes that express individuality to air fresheners that transform a room and liquid care products that make everyday routines more enjoyable, Aura Source brings fragrance into the moments that matter.

OUR STORY

Inspired by the language of scent.

Fragrance has the remarkable ability to change how we experience a person, a place or a moment.

A familiar scent can bring back a memory. A fresh room can change the feeling of an entire day. A signature perfume can become part of someone's identity.

Aura Source was created around this simple belief: everyday fragrance should feel meaningful.

We are developing a brand that combines contemporary design, carefully considered fragrances and accessible everyday products for customers in Nigeria and beyond.

OUR MISSION

To make beautiful fragrance part of everyday life.

We aim to create quality fragrance and care products that combine thoughtful formulation, beautiful presentation and memorable experiences.
