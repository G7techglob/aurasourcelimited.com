1. Introduction

Aura Source Limited ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or purchase our products, including perfumes, liquid soaps, and air fresheners.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information: Your name, email address, telephone number, and delivery address when you provide them.
  • Order information: Products ordered, order history, delivery details, and transaction references where applicable.
  • Messages: Information you provide when contacting our customer support team.
  • Technical information: Browser type, device information, IP address, and website usage information where collected.

We only intend to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and customer requests.
  • Process orders and coordinate deliveries.
  • Provide product information and customer support.
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • Maintain business records and prevent fraud.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Send promotional messages where permitted and, when required, with your consent.

4. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functions, remember preferences, or understand website performance if such tools are enabled.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features. Where required by law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information only when reasonably necessary with:

  • Delivery companies fulfilling your orders.
  • Payment providers processing transactions.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Professional advisers where appropriate.
  • Public authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Where appropriate, we take steps to ensure that service providers handle information securely and only for authorised purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including fulfilling orders, maintaining records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise an applicable right, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices, website features, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date where appropriate.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact Aura Source Limited.
